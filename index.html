Simple process

From search to first lesson
in three steps

Whether you are a parent looking for a tutor or a teacher building your student base — Minerva gets you there fast.

1

Search and filter teachers

Browse by subject, level, language, availability, and budget. Every teacher profile shows real reviews, qualifications, and verification status.

2

Book with escrow protection

Select a time slot and pay securely. Your payment is held in escrow and only released to the teacher after the lesson is complete.

3

Track progress with AI

Get AI-generated lesson summaries and progress dashboards after every session. Identify skill gaps early and forecast exam outcomes.

Platform features

Everything you need for
worry-free learning

Built for Hong Kong families and professional tutors — with the safety guarantees and automation that matter.

Verified teachers only

Every teacher undergoes ID verification, background checks, and reference validation before listing on Minerva.

Escrow payments

Payment is held securely until after the lesson. No risk of losing money if a session is cancelled or unsatisfactory.

AI progress tracking

After every lesson, receive an AI-generated summary — topics covered, skill gaps identified, and predicted HKDSE outcomes.

Live calendar booking

Teachers publish their real-time availability. Parents and students book instantly — no back-and-forth messaging.

Multilingual support

Interface in English, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese. Cantonese, Mandarin, and English teachers.

In-app messaging

Communicate with your teacher directly through the platform. Share lesson notes, files, and feedback without exchanging contacts.

Safety you can trust

Every Minerva teacher is ID-verified, background-checked, and reference-confirmed before they can accept students.

Visible progress

After every lesson, our AI generates a summary — what was taught, skill gaps, and how the student is tracking toward exam goals.

Fair pricing

No agency markups. Teachers set their own rates. Escrow means you only pay for lessons that actually happen.

  • Verified, background-checked teachers
  • Escrow payment protection on every booking
  • AI-generated progress reports after every lesson
  • Live calendar with instant booking
  • Multilingual: English, Cantonese, Mandarin

Keep more of your earnings

Traditional agencies take 30-40% of your fee. On Minerva, you keep 85%+. The platform fee is transparent and only charged per completed lesson.

Student discovery

Get matched with parents actively searching for your subject and level. Build your reputation with verified reviews from real students.

Automated scheduling

Publish your availability once. Students book automatically. No more scheduling via WhatsApp — everything on the platform.
